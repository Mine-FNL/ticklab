## How to Use This List

- **Personalize every message.** A 2-sentence opener about their last piece of work beats a generic "love what you do" every time.
- **Lead with the validation harness, not the marketing pitch.** Most of these audiences can smell "project announcement" from a mile away.
- **For academics: emphasize methodology.** The NORTH_STAR_REPORT.md and the `validate:northstar` script are the parts they care about.
- **For YouTubers: have a 90-second demo ready.** They won't read a README before filming.
- **For newsletters: pitch the angle, not the project.** "An LP simulator that publishes its own error" is the angle. The project is the vehicle.
